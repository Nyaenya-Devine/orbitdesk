### HOW TO RECORD (so you sound human, not AI)

1. **Setup:** Quiet room, noise-canceling headset, close windows, phone on silent. Use https://online-voice-recorder.com/ — test mic first.
2. **Smile while talking:** It changes your voice. For the humour line, actually smile.
3. **Pacing:** Don't rush. Pause after "Hi, I'm Devine from Nairobi." and after "Not a robotic recording, like real calls work."
4. **Emphasis:** Stress words: *ownership*, *simple steps*, *feel heard*, *grow with your international team*
5. **No reading sound:** Read it 3 times out loud first, then record without looking word-for-word — glance, then speak like you're explaining to a friend. It's okay to say "twenty four seven" instead of "24/7".
6. **Humour tip:** When you say "add a little humour to defuse stress" — light chuckle is okay, but don't force joke. Influx wants sense of humour, not comedian.
7. **End strong:** Last sentence slower, warm: "I'd love to grow with your international team." + slight smile.
8. **File:** Save as MP3, name: `Devine_Nyaenya_Why_Influx.mp3`, check size <5MB. Listen once — if you hear background noise, re-record.

### What NOT to say

- ❌ "I badly want to be amongst your group" (desperate)
- ❌ Too much jargon: "SLAs, CSAT, Entra ID, Conditional Access" without simple explanation
- ❌ "I even created a lab please coach me" (begging)
- ✅ Do say: ownership, empathy, simple steps, team player, continuous learner, 44h/week, laptop/headset, G Suite
